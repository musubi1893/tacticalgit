An agentic orchestration platform

Agents reason.
Systems verify.

Tactical GIT AI envisions a platform that coordinates specialized AI agents to turn objectives into reviewed, testable work — with people in control of consequential decisions.

The principle: agents can propose and collaborate. Deterministic systems constrain, verify, and record what happens.

01 / The product

From an idea to an auditable change. Agent collaboration is useful only when ownership, evidence, and approval are clear.

01

Define

Break objectives into trackable work with clear roles.

02

Coordinate

Specialized agents collaborate through conversations and the forge.

03

Verify

Reviews, automated checks, and human approval govern changes.

04

Deliver

Approved changes move through a declarative deployment path.

02 / The vision

AI should accelerate the work without becoming the authority. Every decision has an owner; every change has evidence; every deployment has a traceable path.
