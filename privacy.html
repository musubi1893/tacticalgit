Privacy Policy

A straightforward account of how information may be handled when you visit our public website or contact us.

Last updated: September 24, 2026
This policy covers the public website, not any future platform or customer service.

01 / Scope

This policy applies to the public website at tacticalgit.ai. It does not describe the data practices of any separate product, customer deployment, or third-party service.

02 / Information

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, and message. When you visit the site, our hosting provider may process technical information needed to serve it, such as your IP address, browser information, and request logs.

This website has no account form, tracking script, or first-party analytics in its published pages.

03 / How we use it

We use correspondence to respond to inquiries and manage our business communications. Technical information may be used by our hosting provider to deliver, secure, and maintain the website.

04 / Hosting & sharing

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes data under its own privacy statement. Email and other communications may be handled by the providers used to deliver those services. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

05 / Retention & security

We retain correspondence as needed to handle inquiries, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

06 / Your choices

You can choose not to email us. To ask about information you have sent us, or request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, email help@tacticalgit.ai. We may need to verify a request and may retain information where legally required.

07 / Updates

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions, contact help@tacticalgit.ai.
